Face gestures, redesigned

A new layout for the Face gestures dialog (src/face/dialog.ts): a list of the gestures, and a details view for each, where its channels can be turned off and their thresholds moved.

The mockups are still pictures, caught at a moment while you play and wink. The gestures and numbers are examples. This version takes in your notes: the page counter is gone, the direction stays a dropdown on the list, Record and Rename are on the list, Hold is one setting for every gesture, Release always follows On, and each kind of threshold has its own handle.

  • The camera preview and the face button, ring and all, as the reader shows it. The page counter is gone.

The three kinds of threshold

A gesture's template sets a threshold on each of its channels, of one of three kinds. Today all three are a number in the Details table, with only ≥ or ≤ to tell them apart. Here each kind gets its own handle.

At least

A channel that rises when you do the gesture. Below its threshold it holds the score back, all the way at its rest value; at the threshold or above it, it doesn't.

Blink, right eye: at least 0.58.

At most

A channel that falls when you do the gesture. The same, mirrored: above its threshold it holds the score back; at the threshold or below it, it doesn't.

Eyes wide, right: at most 0.08 (it rests around 0.20).

Stay under (a cap)

The other side of a pair, so the gesture isn't also set off by doing it on both sides. Up to the cap, no effect. From there the score is cut, more the further it goes, until at the moving side's threshold the score is 0.

Blink, left eye: stay under 0.39; score 0 by 0.58.

A gesture's details

  • The header: ‹ back to the list, the name, its direction, and Remove (asks first).
  • What is wrong, at the top, in full words, with what to do about it.
  • On level: drag On on the meter. Release is drawn dashed and can't be dragged: it's always halfway between the rest level and On. Under the meter: the rest level, and each rep's peak; one under On is in the warning colour, since that rep, done that way, wouldn't have turned the page. Fitted level puts On back.
  • Channels, in place of the Details table, one of each kind here. Each row: a switch, the name in words with the raw name under it, a bar with the rest's range hatched, the threshold's handle, and the value now as a dot. The shaded part of the bar is where the channel needs to be (at least, at most); for a cap it's where it cuts the score, darkening to where the score is 0.
  • Brow down, right is switched off in the example: frowning while sight-reading kept reaching it.
  • Trials: the count, Clear trials, Record 5 trials.

What changing a channel does

Turning a channel off fits the gesture again from its trials, with that channel left out. A cap whose moving channel is turned off goes with it. The next clearest channel may take the freed place among the ten kept (MAX_SUPPORT, src/face/_template.ts), and appears as a new row.

Moving a threshold does the same, with that threshold fixed where you put it. While you drag, the meters and the ring score with the new threshold at once; the fit runs again when you let go.

Either one moves the rest level and the reps' peaks, so On goes back to the fitted level, and Release with it. Set the channels first, then On.

Channel changes are kept across rounds and Clear trials; Reset channels drops them.

Recording

From Record 5 trials, on the list or in the details. The same instructions, tones and rules as now, plus:

  • Five dots for the round, filled as each trial is kept.
  • A timeline of the trial under way: the six tones (the third of each set heavier), the held part shaded, and a playhead.
  • For a gesture already fitted, its meter. Nothing turns, as now.

What is stored, and what is dropped

  • Hold moves from each gesture to the settings: FaceSettings gains hold, 500 ms to start. Each gesture's own hold is dropped.
  • Release is no longer stored: it's worked out from the rest level and On. Releases set by hand are dropped; an On set by hand is kept.
  • A gesture gains channels?: { off: string[]; at: Record<string, number> }, its channel changes by channel name. build(trials) becomes build(trials, channels?), and fit takes the channels left out and the thresholds fixed.
  • A gesture with no channel changes fits exactly as before, so FIT_VERSION stays.

Decisions made here, to overrule

  1. Rename and Record 5 trials are buttons on every row of the list; Clear trials and Remove are only in the details.
  2. The details also have the direction, so it can be changed while tuning.
  3. On is set on the meter, and the On and Release sliders go.
  4. Changing a channel fits the gesture again and puts On back to the fitted level.
  5. A channel you turn off can be replaced by the next clearest one.
  6. Channel changes survive new rounds and Clear trials; only Reset channels drops them.
  7. You can turn channels off and move them, but can't add a channel the fit left out.
  8. The Details table's numbers (how clear, the rest's reach, each rep's peak) go; the hatching and the ticks show them instead.
  9. Channels get names in words, with the raw name under each.
  10. A cap's handle and shading are in the warning colour, to set it apart from the thresholds a gesture has to reach.

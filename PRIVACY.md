# Privacy policy

Volti is a sheet music reader that runs in your browser. It has no server of its own: it keeps your
scores, setlists and settings in your browser's storage on your device, and sends them nowhere.

## Google Drive

If you link a Google Drive folder, Volti asks Google for access to your Drive and uses it only to:

- list the files in the folder you linked, and the folders inside it
- download those files to show them, and keep copies on your device so they open offline
- upload, rename, move and send to Drive's bin the files in that folder, when you ask it to

Volti doesn't read or change anything outside the folder you linked. The key that lets Volti reach
your Drive is kept in your browser on your device. Your files go only between your device and
Google: never to the developer or anyone else.

Volti's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Other services

Volti is served by GitHub Pages, which may log the requests your browser makes to download the app.
Volti has no analytics, ads or tracking.
